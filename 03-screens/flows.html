  1. 24
    24. No password to forget

    A passkey first, an email link second (WCAG 3.3.8: no memory or puzzle test). Nothing is asked before the value is shown.

  2. 25
    25. One question per screen

    Option cards are native radios: the whole card is the target, arrows move the choice. Maintain is preselected; nothing extreme is offered.

  3. 26
    26. Say why we ask

    Body data is only for the formula and never shared. “Prefer not to say” uses the average of both formulas; errors say the valid range.

  4. 27
    27. Allergies are a hard filter

    14 major allergens as toggle chips, with “always check labels”. Foods you'd rather skip only move recipes down.

  5. 28
    28. A target that's safe

    The maths is shown (Mifflin-St Jeor × activity). −50 steps stop at 1,550 kcal with a calm note; the macros always add up.

  6. 29
    29. The first day has one next step

    Ring 0 of 2,050, four Add buttons, and “Snap your first meal” with Open camera or Search instead.

Calculate calories in a dish or product

  1. 1
    1. Camera is never the only way in

    Plate guide, three capture tips and a live hint. Library and Search sit next to the shutter.

  2. 2
    2. Honest loading

    Plain steps and Cancel, no fake percentage. Nothing is saved yet, and the primary button says so.

  3. 3
    3. Confidence on every food

    Icon + word: High, Check portion, Not sure. Never colour alone.

  4. 4
    4. One concrete question

    “Check portion” tints the row and asks “Is 150 g right?”. “Not sure” offers the two likely answers and Search.

  5. 5
    5. The button carries the result

    “Add to Lunch · 559 kcal”. Meal is preselected from the time; the estimate range is stated.

  6. 6
    6. Undo, not “Are you sure?”

    Logging is reversible, so it's one tap plus Undo. The toast has no timer.

  7. 7
    7. Two taps from search

    Recents first; one canonical entry per food. The USDA source is named on the food detail (research insight 2).

  8. 8
    8. Per 100 g + source

    The anchor value and its source stay on top; quick portions like “30 g · 1 handful”.

  9. 9
    9. Cooked weight fixes home cooking

    Raw ingredients + the cooked pot weight → real kcal per 100 g and per portion (insight 1).

  1. 10
    10. Lead with what's left

    The ring answers “can I have dinner?”: 879 kcal left, and a recipe that fits it.

  2. 11
    11. “Suits me” is visible

    Diet and allergy come from onboarding. The allergy is a locked chip (lock + “Allergy”) and can only change in Profile.

  3. 12
    12. Ranked by fit, explained in words

    Fits the kcal left → covers the protein gap → fewer kcal. “Fits: 462 of 879 kcal”. One text column: title, time, reason, P/F/C, fit, kcal.

  4. 13
    13. Safety before the numbers

    Allergens in words above the fold; per-portion kcal and macros add up from the ingredients.

  5. 14
    14. Log without leaving

    A bottom sheet: meal preselected, portions, the same macro tiles, one primary button.

  6. 15
    15. Calm when over

    Protein is 10 g over: Turmeric and the words “10 over”. No red, no warning icon.

  7. 16
    16. Fix a dish in place

    Edit turns each row into labelled fields: food, amount, unit. Kcal, the ring and the macro bars update as you type, and screen readers hear the new total.

  8. 17
    17. Delete with Undo

    Focus moves to the next ingredient, so keyboard users don't get lost. The toast offers Undo and has no timer.

  9. 18
    18. Don't lose work

    Leaving with unsaved changes asks “Discard changes?”, with “Keep editing” first.

  10. 19
    19. Errors say how to fix them

    Empty or too-long names get a message with an example; the field keeps its visible label and a live character count.

  11. 20
    20. A real “View recipe” button

    Secondary button, 12.8:1, 44 tall, named “View recipe: Baked cod, potatoes & broccoli”. Enter, Space and click open the dish. The photo opens it too, for pointer users only (no extra tab stop).

  12. 21
    21. The method is part of the dish

    Numbered steps in an ordered list after Ingredients: 16 px text, line height 1.5, ≤ 80 characters a line. Time and servings above, in the card style.

  13. 22
    22. Reorder without dragging

    Move up / Move down buttons (WCAG 2.5.7) keep focus on the moved step and announce its new position. Disabled at the ends, not hidden.

  14. 23
    23. Never a blank section

    A recipe without steps shows “No steps yet” with “Add steps”, which opens a focused Step 1 field.
