**Enabling GitHub Pages:**
1. Merge the work into `main`.
2. In the repository, open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch** → `main` → `/ (root)`.

The site appears at the address above after a minute or two. On 2 October 2026 the address returned 404, because Pages wasn't enabled yet.

The links work from incognito: the site needs no sign-in and loads only the repository files and Google Fonts.
