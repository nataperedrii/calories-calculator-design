Design project · mobile app for calculating calories

Point, snap, know.

Ripe is a calorie calculator that starts with a photo. Point the phone at your plate: Ripe finds each food, estimates the portion and says how sure it is. You check, tap once, and get back to your meal. Every number is real USDA data per 100 g.

  • 1 · Calculate calories in a dish or productFrom a photo, a search or barcode, or a home-cooked dish with its cooked weight.
  • 2 · Find recipes that suit meRanked by what fits the calories and protein left today, filtered by diet and allergies.
